Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Ba-mba (“we”, “us”) is a private network for Israeli MBA students and alumni of business schools in the US and Europe, at ba-mba.com. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We don’t sell your data and we don’t show ads.

What we collect

  • Your profile: first and last name, headline, location, industry, “about” text, schools and class years, work history, optional Israeli background (army unit, Israeli university, hometown), topics, your LinkedIn profile address, and your photo.
  • Your contact details: your email address, and your WhatsApp number if you add one. You choose whether other members see your email and/or number.
  • Your join request: your answer to the joining question, and whether you signed up with a LinkedIn PDF.
  • What you do on Ba-mba: feed posts and post photos, people you star (only you see these), “interested” marks on events, intro requests, a short status note, and reports you send to the admins.
  • From LinkedIn, if you sign in with it: LinkedIn shares your name, email address and profile photo. We keep your LinkedIn name to confirm the profile is yours and your email to log you in. We don’t receive or store your connections, messages or anything else, and we never post on LinkedIn for you.
  • Your LinkedIn PDF: if you upload your LinkedIn “Save to PDF” file, it is read in your own browser to fill in the sign-up form. The file itself is never sent to us or stored. Only the details you review and submit are saved.
  • Technical data: your login session is kept in your browser so you stay logged in. When someone checks whether an email is already registered, we briefly keep their IP address (deleted within a day) to stop abuse. Our hosting providers keep standard server logs.

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To review your request to join. An admin approves every member by hand.
  • To show your profile to other approved members, so they can find you and reach out.
  • To send service emails: sign-up codes, “we got your request”, approval and welcome emails, intro notifications and password resets.
  • To keep the network safe: check that sign-ups are real, handle reports, and prevent spam.

Who can see your information

  • Approved members see your profile, photo, posts and status note, plus the contact details you chose to share.
  • Admins also see join requests (including your answer and email), reports, and whether you signed in with LinkedIn.
  • Nobody who isn’t logged in and approved can see members’ profiles. Pending applicants see only their own profile.
  • Reports are private: the person you report never learns who reported them.

Service providers

We use a few companies to run Ba-mba. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database, logins and photo storage.
  • Vercel: hosting the website.
  • Google: sending our emails (Gmail) and the website’s fonts (Google Fonts).
  • Cloudflare: our domain name.
  • LinkedIn: “Sign in with LinkedIn”.
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs: deliver two open-source code libraries the site loads.

Some of these providers may store data outside your country, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, contact details, schools, work history, posts, stars, intros, photos and status note are deleted right away. Decisions admins made about other members, and anonymous server logs kept by our providers, may remain for a limited time.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: view and edit your profile and contact details anytime in the app.
  • Control your contact details: choose in Settings whether members see your email and/or WhatsApp number.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account removes your data for good.
  • Questions, a copy of your data, or other requests (including under GDPR or other privacy laws): email us at the address below. We’ll reply within 30 days.
  • LinkedIn: you can also remove Ba-mba’s access in your LinkedIn settings (Data privacy → Permitted services).

Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to data is enforced by the database itself, not just the website: members can only read what this policy describes, and only you can change your own profile. Photos are kept in private storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell affected members promptly if something goes wrong.

Age

Ba-mba is for adults (18+) who study or studied in an MBA program.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we’ll update the date above and let members know in the app or by email.
